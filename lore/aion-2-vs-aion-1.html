AION 2 vs AION 1 — What Changed?

🔄 Sequel, not reboot · For returning players · Ashura Whole Heavens

Played the original AION and wondering what's different this time? AION 2 is a true sequel, not a remake — same world, same factions, but a new era, a new engine, and a fresh start on gear and story. Here's what's changed for returning players.

🌀

📜 Story: 200 years later

AION 2 is set roughly two centuries after the original, after the collapse of the Tower of Eternity. Crucially, it's a continuation, not a reset — the events of AION 1 happened, and their consequences shape the sequel. The biggest shift: the Daevas are losing their power, the once-dominant winged immortals now in decline. The Elyos–Asmodian war still burns and the Balaur still threaten everyone.

🖥️ Engine & platform

The original AION ran on CryENGINE; AION 2 is built on Unreal Engine 5, bringing a major visual and technical leap. It's also designed as a cross-platform MMORPG — releasing on PC and mobile — a big departure from the PC-only original.

⚔️ Factions: familiar, with a redesign

The two playable factions are exactly what veterans expect: Elyos and Asmodians, with the Balaur returning as the NPC antagonist faction. But there's a notable art-direction change — to move away from a simple "good vs evil" read, the Asmodians got a sleeker, more attractive redesign rather than the overtly demonic look of old. Both sides are meant to feel appealing to play.

The core faction identity is intact — light vs shadow — but AION 2 wants you to want to be an Asmodian, not just tolerate the demon look.
🌀

🎨 Customization & wings

AION 2 leans hard into character creation — reportedly 200+ adjustable features, with iconic body types from the original (including the fan-favorite Chibi and Big Head options) returning alongside new ones. As always, your wings are fully customizable — your ascension, personalized.

🗺️ World & combat feel

AION 2 aims for a large, more seamless open world with modern action-oriented combat, versus the more tab-target, zone-gated feel of the 2009 original. Flight, the Abyss, and faction warfare remain central — but the moment-to-moment gameplay is built for today's MMO expectations.

✅ Quick recap for returning players

  • Same world (Atreia), same war — Elyos vs Asmodian vs Balaur.
  • New era — ~200 years later; Daevas are fading.
  • New engine — Unreal Engine 5, PC + mobile cross-play.
  • Redesigned Asmodians — sleeker, less overtly demonic.
  • Deeper customization — 200+ features, returning body types, custom wings.
  • Fresh start — new progression, new gear; your old AION 1 characters don't carry over.
