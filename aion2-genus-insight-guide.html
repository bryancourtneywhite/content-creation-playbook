🐾 What Is Genus Insight? (the system most players miss)

Genus Insight is the pet-progression layer that turns your pet collection into permanent character stats. Every pet you own sorts into one of five genus boards:

  • Cogni
  • Fera
  • Natura
  • Varian
  • Special (unlocked through special mount types)

Each board levels on its own as you collect and level pets within that genus. Every board level opens a roll slot, up to 9 slots per board. You roll stat bonuses into those slots using Soul Crystals and Kinah, and the stats apply whether or not the pet is summoned. That means it's pure passive Combat Power and damage sitting on top of your gear, with zero gear investment required.

💡 Why most players get this wrong: they either skip Genus Insight entirely (free CP just sitting there), or they lock their rolls too early and waste Soul Crystals and Kinah on stats they'll replace later. The other classic mistake is dumping everything into one rare pet instead of spreading levels across many easy pets first.

Here's the proof it matters: on my heroic Genus Insight setup (set 3), I'm at 186K CP in PvE, and I'll be 200K+ next week once I swap out my weapons. That's 5K optimized Combat Power you're throwing away, and I'm not even fully finished with my insight yet.

The point isn't just raw CP, though. It's getting the right Combat Power spike, meaning you're optimizing your stats for your build instead of just stacking whatever the board hands you.

📈 Step 1: Level Your Pets (the secret to fast leveling)

Before you touch genus insight rolling, you need to level your pets. Each pet you level per genus type grants owned effects (passive stats that stack up across your collection). Here's how the levels break down:

Pet Level Breakpoints
  • Level 1: you gain HP (for the Cogni genus). [VERIFY FROM VIDEO: exact per-genus level 1 stat]
  • Level 2: you get HP and a little crit. It's okay, but not the real payoff.
  • Level 3: you unlock the main stat (crit and might for Cogni). This is the level you want every pet to hit. [VERIFY FROM VIDEO: confirm might is the Cogni level 3 stat]
🎯 Get every pet to level 3. That's where the main stat unlocks. Level 2 only gives a little crit, so it's not worth stopping there. The same pattern holds for every genus type, just with different stats at level 3.
Main Stat Per Genus at Level 3 [VERIFY FROM VIDEO]
  • Cogni: HP, crit, and might
  • Fera: accuracy and dexterity
  • Natura: crit resist and precision
  • Varian: evasion and constitution
  • Special: the best-in-slot stats for the special genus insight, unlocked once you get a special mount type
The Secret to Fast Pet Leveling

Most people know about Ascension Trial, which gives you growth pets. The pet dungeon used to give you pets, but now it gives you crystals. Those crystals are good for leveling genus insight, not for leveling pets. So what's the actual secret to leveling pets?

  1. Use NotMeter. When you open NotMeter and look at your pet list, it tells you every pet that still needs to be leveled to 3. [VERIFY FROM VIDEO: confirm the NotMeter feature name and behavior] This is your tracking tool so you're not guessing.
  2. Know your faction's pets. Pets are split by faction (Elios vs Asmodian). The pets on your side are the ones you can get. Level all the pets available on your faction side to 3 first.
  3. Then buy from the Black Cloud Merchant (covered in Step 2) to fill in the gaps your faction doesn't give you.
🐾 NotMeter does the work for you. Instead of manually checking every pet, open NotMeter and it lists exactly which pets still need to reach level 3. Across all your characters, you can see at a glance what's left to level.

🛒 Step 2: Buy Pets Smart (Black Cloud Merchant & bound Kinah)

Once you've leveled all the pets available on your faction side, the next move is buying pets from the Black Cloud Merchant. Here's the efficient method I use across my five characters:

The Alt-Swapping Method
  1. Swap between every character and check the Black Cloud Merchant shop on each one. The shop inventory can differ, so you're scanning for pets (and skins) you don't have yet.
  2. Only spend bound Kinah on your alts. Bound Kinah is Kinah that can't be transferred to your main, so it's the perfect currency for alt shopping. If you need more, sell celentium on the alt for a little Kinah. [VERIFY FROM VIDEO: confirm celentium is the item sold for bound Kinah]
  3. Only buy skins below 200K. A 700K skin is kind of a ripoff, so set your ceiling and stick to it. [VERIFY FROM VIDEO: confirm the 200K skin price ceiling and 700K ripoff threshold]
💲 Bound Kinah only on alts. The key rule is never spend transferable Kinah on an alt when you could send it to your main. Use the Kinah that's stuck on the alt (bound) for all your pet and skin purchases. That way every Kinah you spend is Kinah you couldn't use on your main anyway.
Same Method for Genus Insight Rolling

I use the exact same alt-swapping, bound-Kinah-only method for rolling genus insight. When I have 900K bound Kinah on an alt, I roll genus insight on that alt instead of wasting main-account Kinah. [VERIFY FROM VIDEO: confirm the 900K figure and that genus insight rolling can be done on alts]

💡 Lock only the most important stats. This is your first major Kinah sink, so treat it with respect. Lock only the stats with unique 50% max values, and don't fritter Kinah locking filler stats. Every lock costs Kinah, so save it for the stats that actually move your build forward.
🚀 Level the genus to 10 (max) as soon as you can. Higher board levels give better stat values on your rolls, so pushing every board toward 10 before you start locking gives you stronger rolls to choose from.

🎲 Step 4: The Rolling Order (yellow vs heroic, don't lock early)

Now for the order of operations, which is where most players burn their resources. Here's how I think about genus insight setups:

Yellow (Rare) vs Heroic Setup

I went for a yellow (rare) setup really early on because it was cheap. And in the current state of the game, if you're pushing in-game content, it's probably better to just go straight for heroic. But here's the catch: you can't get a heroic setup until genus board level 10. [VERIFY FROM VIDEO: confirm heroic unlocks at board level 10]

📊 The numbers: my yellow (rare) setup gives about 76.3K CP from genus insight alone. My heroic setup gives about 77.49K CP. Keep in mind, even though it's only a couple thousand Combat Power, this is CP that's optimized because I'm getting best-in-slot stats, not just raw numbers. [VERIFY FROM VIDEO: confirm 76.3K yellow and 77.49K heroic figures]

If you're really low on Genus Insight, sometimes it's nice to have a yellow setup early, and you can level your Genus Insight in another preset. But once you can go heroic, go heroic.

Rolling Priority: Finish Your Weakest Slots First

Here's the key trick. Look at your current genus insight board. I had two blues, one green, a yellow, and a white on one of my boards. In order of priority, the most beneficial move was to finish by rolling both of the lower-rarity slots to heroic, looking for the best stats for those two slots. ⚠️ Don't lock too early. The single biggest mistake players make with genus insight is locking rolls before the board is leveled. You lock a stat, then you level the board, and now you're stuck with a sub-optimal roll you paid Kinah to lock. The right order is: unlock many easy pets, level every board toward 10, THEN start rolling and locking your best stats.
The Order of Operations
  1. Unlock many easy pets first (level them all to 3 for the owned effects).
  2. Level every genus board toward 10 (max level for better stat values).
  3. Roll your slots looking for the best-in-slot stats per slot (see Step 3).
  4. Lock only the 50% max-value stats you want to keep, starting with the highest-priority slots (4, 7, 2, 8).
  5. Upgrade lower-rarity slots to heroic once you can, rather than wasting resources locking filler on a blue or green slot.
🐾 Spread levels, don't dump. The other classic mistake is dumping everything into one rare pet. Spreading your levels across many easy pets gives you more owned effects across more boards, which compounds into more total CP than focusing on a single pet.
The Genus Insight Checklist:
  1. Don't skip Genus Insight. It's free passive CP and DMG on top of your gear, zero gear upgrades required.
  2. Level every pet to 3 to unlock the main stat (might for Cogni, accuracy/dexterity for Fera, crit resist/precision for Natura, evasion/constitution for Varian).
  3. Use NotMeter to track which pets still need leveling across all your characters.
  4. Max your faction's pets first, then buy from the Black Cloud Merchant to fill gaps.
  5. Only spend bound Kinah on alts for pet and skin purchases. Only buy skins below 200K.
  6. Level every genus board toward 10 (max) before you start locking, for better stat values.
  7. Roll for best-in-slot stats per slot (see the stat table in Step 3). Priority slots: 4, 7, 2, 8.
  8. Lock only 50% max-value stats and don't fritter Kinah on filler.
  9. Don't lock too early. Unlock many pets, level boards to 10, THEN roll and lock. Never lock before the board is leveled.
  10. Go straight for heroic if you're pushing content (unlocks at board level 10). Yellow (rare) is a cheap early stopgap only.
  11. Spread levels across many pets, don't dump everything into one rare pet.
