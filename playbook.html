The Workflow

Every video, start to finish

Follow these in order. Each step feeds the next, so the whole thing becomes muscle memory.

1🎥

Capture Footage SteelSeries 1080p

Record all gameplay at full 1080p using SteelSeries. This is the raw material for both long-form videos and Shorts.

  • Record in 1080p for crisp, upload-ready quality
  • Grab your best moments, clutch plays, and highlights
2✂️

Edit the Video CapCut 9:16 + 16:9

Build the video in CapCut. Shorts are cut to 1080p in vertical 9:16 format; long-form videos stay in 1080p widescreen.

  • Shorts: 1080p, 9:16 vertical
  • Long-form: 1080p widescreen
  • Turn on auto-captions and export the transcript for later timestamps
3⬆️

Upload to YouTube YouTube

Upload the finished Short or long-form video to the channel. This is where the optimization work begins.

  • Shorts uploaded to YouTube Shorts
  • Long-form uploaded as standard videos
4🧪

A/B Test the Title YouTube Studio

Set up an A/B test on the video title using YouTube's Test & Compare. Let the data pick the title that pulls the most views.

  • Write 2-3 title variations
  • Let YouTube rotate them and crown the winner
5📝

Write the Description + Timestamps

Craft a description that matches the video summary. Add chapter timestamps whenever the video is long enough to have distinct sections.

  • Summarize what the video is about
  • Add timestamps / chapters when applicable
6🖼️

Design the Thumbnail VidIQ

Create a thumbnail in VidIQ that matches the channel's theme and the video's subject. Consistency builds a recognizable brand.

  • On-brand colors, fonts, and layout every time
  • Reflect the specific subject of the video
7🏷️

Tags & Category YouTube

Add relevant tags and file the video under the Gaming category so it reaches the right audience and surfaces in the right places.

  • Add targeted, relevant tags
  • Set category to Gaming

Level Up

Habits that get you to the front page

The workflow gets the video out. These habits get it seen.

🎯

Reuse templates

Keep a saved CapCut template for Shorts and one for long-form so editing is fill-in-the-blank, not from scratch.

🔁

Always A/B test

Never settle on a single title. Let the Test & Compare data decide which one earns the click.

🎨

Brand consistency

Same thumbnail style every time. Viewers should recognize your video before they read the title.

⏱️

Timestamps matter

Chapters keep viewers watching longer and help YouTube understand your content.
