⚔️ AION 2 — HOW TO PLAY

The AION 2 Player's Guide

Everything a new or returning player needs to climb fast — the free-to-play progression path from level 1 to 2,500 combat power, straight from a Rank 1 player who did it without ever swiping. 🌙

① Leveling 1–45 ② First Gear (Draft) ③ PvP Gear Trick ④ Farming AP ⑤ 2,000→2,500 CP ⑥ Arcana & Endgame
💡 The core idea: AION 2 is full of resource sinks that keep you weak — wasted kinah, wasted materials, wasted time on the wrong content. This guide is about optimizing every resource so you climb combat power fast, especially as a free-to-play player. The concepts apply to everyone; F2P players just have to be more precise.
①

Leveling 1 → 45: do everything on the map

Follow the yellow main-story quests — they route you through the world and unlock the systems you need. As you go:

  • Do every green quest. They hand you gear that eases leveling — and when you outgrow it, dissolve it for enhancement stones and materials.
  • Clear the map's question marks. Those undiscovered spots are sealed dungeons and strongholds. If the story walks you past one, do it — you'll have to clear them all eventually.
  • They pay out statue scraps, titles, enhancement stones, Daeva crystals, and kinah — the essential mats for combat power.
🎯 At level 45: go back and finish every green quest, sealed dungeon, and stronghold you skipped. That alone puts you around 1,100–1,200 combat power.
②

First real gear: farm Draft, not Crowd Cave

Open your Expedition menu and run Draft (Draftnear). Completing dungeons the required number of times gives you a free pity item from the condensed cube — your first entry-level unique (~57 item level), the Transcendent Breastplate.

  • Chase the Barma gear (68 item level) from Draftnear — it's so good it's still usable at 2,500 CP.
  • Skip Crowd Cave. Its unique isn't a big enough upgrade and costs too many mats. Instead, use the gold unique weapon from the main-story ascension quest — it lasts you until ~1,700 CP.
  • Prioritize armor from Draft (breastplate + legs are the best), since your weapon is handled by the quest reward.
📈 From ~1,100 CP, farm Draft up to about 1,700 CP — but don't burn all your tickets here. Save energy for Canyon later.
③

The PvP-gear trick to break 1,800+ CP

Here's the biggest F2P shortcut right now: PvP gear is nearly identical to PvE gear — the PvP piece just has one extra stat line. In PvE the difference is negligible, so you can grind PvP gear to fill the gaps in your PvE setup and push from 1,600 → 1,800 → 2,000 CP.

  • Buy PvP accessories first — they're cheaper and give strong combat power per AP.
  • This is valid until the next season (the guide will be updated when that changes).
  • You have to PvX. Pure PvE players won't have enough resources to gear through all the content — do both.
④

Where to farm AP (Abyss Points)

AP buys that PvP gear. The best sources, in order of efficiency:

  • Command Shop quests — look for the red-text quests (best value at low CP). Many send you to Eternal Isle, where regular mobs can drop uniques while you rack up AP.
  • Duty missions (press J) — daily AP quests with a bonus random reward. Easy guaranteed AP.
  • Battlefield — ~20k AP from your weekly wins. Even if you're the weakest player, you can get carried to the win.
  • Killing players in rifts and the abyss — scales with skill, best once you have some gear.
⛔ Skip Arena for AP. The victory rewards are tiny (~100/win). Play it for the title and season rewards, not combat power.
⑤

2,000 → 2,500 CP: Canyon & the min-max grind

At ~1,800–2,000 CP you can start Canyon (your next weapon) and Sky Island. Canyon pays ~400k, Sky Island ~200k — prioritize Canyon. Then the real min-maxing begins:

  • Shugo Festival — PvE Daeva crystals + skill points.
  • Feathers (open world + abyss) → the Monolith for skill points, keys, and enhancement scrolls.
  • Nightmare dungeons — push the highest tier before each reset.
  • Ascension Trial at 2,000+ for max kinah and season rewards.
  • Statues in your Pantheon → big passive stats. Trade-shop PvE crystals up to step 3 (not past).
⑥

Arcana cards & true endgame

The single most important min-max is your Arcana cards, earned in Transcendence (separate tickets from Expedition — only Odal energy is shared, so spend it wisely).

  • Grind each floor for an S rank to progress. Greens drop around floor 4; guaranteed blues around floors 6–7.
  • As a F2P player, ~floor 46–47 is the realistic stopping point — past that isn't worth it unless you're swiping.
  • Gold card: combine mysterious crystals — 2 from achievements, 2 from the season shop (~50k season rewards each), and 1 from extracting spare cards. The Grail card is the recommended gold craft.
🏰 True endgame: the Sanctuary raid needs ~2,700 CP to enter and 3,000+ to realistically clear. The entry-level raid is mostly just enhancement stones — don't over-invest in it early.
