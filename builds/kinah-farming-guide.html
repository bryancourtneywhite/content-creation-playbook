Farming Kinah Guide

Fastest Gold (Kinah) Farming Method

💰 ~1M kinah / hour · Solo farm · Ashura Whole Heavens
Patch: Launch Meta Updated: Sep 25, 2026

This is the fastest kinah farming method I've found in AION 2 right now — roughly 1 million kinah per hour, solo. No macros, no contested open-world spots, just a repeatable dungeon run.

⚡ The Method (TL;DR)

💰 Run Expedition → Conquest Mode (Draft), solo, on Private. High mob density + easy clears with good AoE and sustain. ~6:38 per run, ~85–86K kinah per run, averaging ~1M kinah/hour.

📋 Step by Step

  1. Open Expedition and select Conquest Mode (Draft) — it has the most mobs and the easiest clears of the dungeons.
  2. Create a room and set it to Private. Do not let anyone join.
  3. Clear packs with AoE, using your sustain to stay topped up between pulls.
  4. Collect the kinah and uniques that drop, finish the run, and re-open a fresh one.
  5. Rinse and repeat — about 2 hours nets ~2M, then swap to another character and keep going alongside your daily expeditions.

🔒 Why Solo & Private

  • More players = harder mobs and less loot for you — the run scales up but your share shrinks.
  • I noticed better drop rates farming solo in general.
  • Solo means all the kinah is yours — no splitting.

🧭 Why This Beats Open-World & Abyss Farming Right Now

  • Abyss PvE farming got capped — past roughly 3rd–5th class rank, the risk of losing while farming isn't worth the reward anymore.
  • Open-world is contested by macro and bot players in every decent zone. Since you often can't PvP them to contest the spot, most good mobs are already claimed — so open-world farming is inefficient at the moment.
  • Conquest Draft sidesteps both: instanced, solo, uncontested, and consistent.
💡 Requirements: you'll want decent AoE damage and some sustain to chain packs without downtime — that's what makes the ~6:38 clear times possible.
Farm smart. Stack kinah. 🌙
