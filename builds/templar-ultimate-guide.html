Templar PvP PvE Ultimate Guide

AION 2 Templar Ultimate Guide (PvP & PvE)

🛡️ Berserk / DPS Templar · 100K+ PvE DPS · Ashura Whole Heavens
Patch: Launch Meta Updated: Sep 25, 2026
🔗 Questlog Build ⌨️ Macro Guide

The complete Templar guide from ~4 weeks maining Templar on the Taiwan server — gear philosophy, best-in-slot stats, arcana cards, David board pathing, stigmas, skills, macros, and full PvP/PvE rotations. I'm hitting 100K+ PvE DPS on the in-game meter. The exact gear won't carry to global (levels/gear/skills get squished for Season 1), but the ideology and rotations translate — that's what makes you a strong Templar at launch.

🛡️ Core thesis: DPS Templar is the god-mode PvX class. Build one PvX set, not separate PvP/PvE sets — chasing perfect dual sets is only for whales. The only real PvP/PvE difference right now is your devotion runes (and maybe the amulet).

🎯 Best-in-Slot Stats

Prioritize damage everywhere. The stats that matter most on Templar:

  • Might and Precision — your two most important stats. If you can't hit, you do no damage; if you can't crit, you do far less; base attack scales everything.
  • Attack, Crit, Accuracy — the repeating core trio across gear. (Precision > Accuracy, but you can only roll Accuracy on non-weapon pieces.)
  • Perfect Chance — enormous for damage. The only thing better than Perfect Chance is Smite.
  • Critical Damage Boost on your shoulder is critical.
  • Watch accuracy thresholds for PvE bosses; in PvP, accuracy has diminishing returns but you still stack it because so many players stack evasion.

Chase your main DPS skills as accessory rolls where possible — Punishment, Judgment, Pummel (top three). Keep block/evasion rolls (either is fine — stacking accuracy raises evasion simultaneously, so your stats synergize). Crit hit resist and block penetration are huge in PvP (block pen lets you kill Templars/clerics/supports).

🃏 Arcana Cards & David Board

  • Path toward your main DPS skills first on the David board — Punishment, Judgment, Pummel — grabbing nearby golden/attack nodes on the way. Economy matters: go for what's near if resources are tight, but always aim at damage.
  • Cards carry your key skills: Judgment on parchment, Pummel/Punishment on chalice, Pummel on compass.
  • Getting Judgment to level 20 and Pummel to level 20 is essential.
  • Frenzy for PvE attack + boss damage boost (PvE > PvP for gearing, even as a PvPer). Flash Rampage falls off past mid-game — don't over-invest.
  • Best card stat targets: Fury (best-in-slot passive), Judgment, Annihilate, Pummel. Aim for 4/5 best-in-slot stats to start leveling; min-max later.

⚔️ Stigmas (PvX Layout)

No block/taunt in this setup — stopping to block loses DPS; keep passive shields (Nezekan Shield, Second Skin) up instead. Priority order:

  1. Poach — reset cooldown (top PvX priority; infinite pulls/resets). For 1v1 arena, swap to 20% status effect resist.
  2. Imperion Lord — third milestone (PvP + PvE damage boost). 200% damage vs incapacitated-immune targets, ranged stun — one of the best skills in the game.
  3. Executing Blade — 2nd milestone important, 4th very important. Puts enemy skills on cooldown and inflicts Exposed (lowers block/evasion).
  4. Debilitating Smash — anti-heal (−16% incoming healing), ignores block/evasion. Key vs self-sustain classes.
  5. Then Warding Strike (shield, block, triggers Judgment), Battlefield Banner, Nezekan Shield, Second Skin (last).

🗡️ Key Skill Specs

  • Judgment — all damage, all the time: extra damage, crit, remove cooldown. Never changes at 20. Your main DPS.
  • Punishment — DoT, +30% skill speed, ignore block/evasion. ~100K per fully-charged hit + 56K DoT. Always prioritize when off cooldown despite its ~30s cooldown.
  • Pummel — needs level 20 for the 12% damage increase + extra Punishing Strike (~13K, basically a free Judgment proc). Slot HP absorb.
  • Annihilate — spam on right click; big damage on stunned/knocked-down targets (~24K base), 50% chance to inflict knockdown in PvP.
  • Doom Shield — resets on defeating an enemy and resets Annihilate on hit → infinite knockdown spam. Put it on right click for PvX rift/open-world; keep it on a separate key for precise 1v1s.

⌨️ Macros

⚠️ Never AFK-macro. Active macro use while playing is fine, but AFK macroing gets you banned. Don't do it.
  • Right click (in-game macro): Doom Shield — the macro fires everything in that slot, so it becomes Annihilate → Pummel spam when they're off cooldown, weaving Vicious Strike into Pummel.
  • Thumb button (Razer HyperSpeed → R key, Turbo ~10 presses/sec): the Judgment macro. It prioritizes Judgment, using Warding Strike before it; Judgment procs from Warding Strike, Shield Smite, and Doom Shield.
  • Goal: keep Judgment spam aligned with your rotation, animation-canceling Judgment into Pummel. This transfers to global.

🔁 Rotations

PvP (soft initiate)

Poach → Shield Smite → strafe to their backside → Judgment + Pummel (animation weave) → big Punishment → Imperion Lord if they flee → Executing Blade when they're low. Learn to strafe behind opponents — more damage, less damage taken, better battlefield visibility.

PvE

Fully buff → Poach in → Punishment → Judgment macro spam → Doom Shield to extend Judgment window → Shield Smite comes back up → back into Judgment spam. Add Executing Blade + Imperion Lord at the end. Always re-prioritize Punishment the moment it's off cooldown. ~300K+ DPS on the combo.

🖥️ Settings That Matter

  • Mass combat quality → Low; post-processing & global illumination → Normal (reduces screen clutter — this game has heavy effects).
  • Camera: lower vertical look speed to ~half your horizontal — you scan left/right far more than up/down; reduces screen shake and keeps you on target.
  • Turn off world-map transition (buggy, can lag you in combat). Consider turning off combat floaters/damage numbers to cut clutter.
  • Hotkeys: C = Quick Slot 2; side mouse buttons for Quick Slot 1 / quick-use; Shift + middle mouse for fly up/down; the R macro key and the right-click in-game macro drive your DPS.
