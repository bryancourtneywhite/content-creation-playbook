🛡️ Equipment & Gear Path

There are three ways to gear for PvE, and which one you pick comes down to how much time you want to sink:

  1. Dungeon weapons, armor & accessories is the casual player path.
  2. Crafted gear is the vertical progression route, and it is what future-proofs your character.
  3. A mix of both is what most people actually run: lean on dungeon gear until you can transition fully into crafted.
🗺️ Recommended path: finish the main quest and the sub-quests that hand you gear, then head straight to the abyss as early as you can to pick up the item level 50+ Holy gear. The Holy set is your real starting point, not the leftovers from questing.

The Holy Spirit pieces are the target here. They sit at item level 55, carry a healthy defense and HP block, and come with 4 manastone slots each, so they are worth binding into properly rather than treating as a throwaway step.

The Upgrade Ladder

Once you have the full Holy set and your stigmas slotted, you should be sitting around 1600 CP. That is the gate. From there the gear path runs like this:

  1. Aulamus Longsword from Urugugu Canyon. This is your first real weapon jump: a Unique longsword at item level 76 with a big attack range (308 to 385), plus Accuracy, Crit, and Block baked in. It binds on pickup, so commit to it once it drops.
  2. Vakron Guard, picked up while you run Vakron Sky Island. A Unique guard at item level 76 that rounds out the Vakron transition.
  3. Keep upgrading your Vakron gear (Breastplate, Greaves, Helm, Pauldrons, Gloves, Boots) until you are strong enough to run Fire Temple.
  4. Fire Temple is your next fork in the road.

Fire Temple and Beyond

When you can clear Fire Temple, you have a choice to make:

  • Spam Fire Temple for the Enraged Kromede extended weapon. This is a Unique (Extended) longsword at item level 84, stepping your attack range up to 340 to 425. It is the payoff for grinding the temple.
  • Farm Ferocious Horn Den for the Nuakum gear set (Breastplate, Greaves, Helm, Pauldrons, Gloves, Boots), your next full armor tier.
🔁 Order of operations: Holy set to 1600 CP, then Aulamus, then Vakron Guard and the rest of the Vakron set, then Fire Temple for the Enraged Kromede weapon while you farm Ferocious Horn Den for the Nuakum set. Each step is a clean upgrade over the last, so you never waste a bind.

The End Goal - Best in Slot

Everything above is the road. This is the destination. Your best-in-slot target splits into two setups: a pure PvE build, and the PvX build I recommend for most players. Pick one before you sink resources into a weapon or a full set you will not use.

Best in Slot - PvE

The pure PvE target. Chase it in this order: weapon first, then guard, then accessories.

  • White Dragon Lord Longsword - your BiS PvE weapon.
  • White Dragon Guard - the matching guard.
  • White Dragon accessories.
  • Nuakum armor for the body pieces.
💡 A note on armor: going for the full White Dragon armor set is really only worth it for whales. Survivability is not the problem for a Templar - damage is - which is exactly why your weapon and accessories come first and Nuakum armor covers the body just fine. Spend your resources where they add damage, not on chasing a full premium armor set you do not need.
Best in Slot - PvX (recommended)

This is the setup I recommend for most players. It keeps you strong in PvE while staying flexible, and it leans on gear you are already farming rather than a separate PvP set. Aim for:

  • Enraged Kromede Longsword as your mainhand.
  • White Dragon Guard for the off-hand.
  • Nuakum armor for the body.
  • White Dragon accessories to finish the setup.
📝 A note on PvP gear: dedicated PvP gear exists in the KR/TW versions (sets that carry a flat PvP Damage Boost), but it is not confirmed for the global build yet, so this guide does not build around it. If a PvP set does make it in, the one rule that always holds is this: get your PvP stigma before you ever spend on a PvP weapon or armor piece. Buying PvP gear ahead of the stigma is the classic way to waste resources you cannot get back. For almost everyone the PvX setup above is the right call regardless.

🪽 Wings

Wings are a straight damage-and-cooldown upgrade, and for Templar the cooldown part is the whole game - less shield-skill cooldown means more Judgment procs. Prioritize accordingly.

Early Game

Run Dark Veil Wings early. They bring strong attack and damage bonuses, which is exactly what you want while you are still building up your gear and combat power.

Late Game / Best in Slot

Talisra Wings are the best-in-slot pick. They carry Cooldown Reduction, the single most important wing stat for Templar, so they are what you transition into for the long haul.

⏱️ Stack Cooldown Reduction as early as possible. Judgment's activation relies on your shield skills - reducing their cooldown means more Judgment procs, which means more damage. This is the same CDR logic that drives the gear and Illusion pantheon choices.

Beyond your equipped pair, collect every wing for its owned effects and flight power - both are very useful for abyss farming.

⌨️ Templar Macro Meta - The Holy Trinity

The meta Templar setup is built on the Holy Trinity: Judgment Punishment Pummel - all three taken to Lv20. With max Judgment, Punishment, and Pummel, you can run the BIS Macro Setup: animation-cancel Judgment into Pummel for maximum sustained DPS, re-prioritizing Punishment the moment it is off cooldown.

🩸 Holy Trinity (BIS core): Judgment Lv20 + Punishment Lv20 + Pummel Lv20. This is the damage backbone every Templar meta build is built around.
BIS Macro Setup - Hotkeys
  • Left Click - Quick Slot 1
  • Right Click - In-Game Macro (Quick Slot 4)
  • C - Quick Slot 2
  • R - Judgment (Razer macro key)
  • Thumb - HyperSpeed → R, ~10 presses/sec
Macro Chains
  • Right Click (PvX): Doom Shield → Annihilate → Debilitating Smash → Pummel
  • Right Click (PvE): Annihilate → Debilitating Smash → Flash Rampage → Pummel
  • Thumb (R): Judgment → Shield Smite → Warding Strike → Doom Shield
⚙️ How the in-game macro works: it activates off the first skill and runs the chain by priority (0 → 1 → 2 → 3), only firing a skill if it is available or procced. So a PvX macro that reads Doom Shield → Annihilate → Debilitating Smash → Pummel becomes a Pummel spam when nothing is procced, and auto-weaves Annihilate (on knockdown) and Debilitating Smash (on proc) when they light up. 📈 Stigma Leveling Priority (Breakpoints)

How you level your stigmas matters as much as which ones you slot. The whole system is driven by breakpoint value - each level milestone on a stigma is worth more or less depending on the game mode, so you chase the breakpoints that pay off for how you are playing.

🔑 The rule: unlock breadth first, then depth by value. Get all 4 stigmas to Lv1 for a game mode before deep-leveling any single one. Then push your most valuable ones to Lv5. Past Lv5, every jump (Lv10 → Lv15 → Lv20 → Lv25) is decided by which breakpoint gives the most value for that mode.

Step 1 - Unlock all 4 (Lv1)

Before anything else, take all four stigmas for the game mode to Lv1. Breadth beats early depth - you want every tool online first.

Step 2 - First pushes to Lv5

Now take your most beneficial stigmas to Lv5, in this order:

  1. Lv5 - Empyrean Lord or Executing Blade (whichever fits your mode - see below).
  2. Lv5 - Battlefield Banner.

Step 3 - Chase breakpoints (Lv10+)

After Lv5, leveling is purely about the value of each breakpoint on your best stigmas. Prioritize in this order, following whichever tags match how you play:

  • Lv10 - Empyrean Lord · PvE Prio
  • Lv10 - Executing Blade · 1v1 PvP Prio
  • Lv10 + Lv15 - Battlefield Banner · PvX Prio
  • Lv15 - Doom Shield · PvX Prio
  • Lv15 - Nezekan Shield · PvX Prio
  • Lv20 - Doom Shield · PvP Prio
  • Lv20 - Empyrean Lord · PvP Prio
  • Lv20 - Comrade in Arms · PvX Prio
  • Lv25 - Executing Blade · 1v1 PvP Prio
  • Lv25 - Doom Shield · PvX Prio
  • Lv25 - Battlefield Banner · PvE Prio
🎯 Read it by mode: if you are grinding PvE, prioritize the PvE-tagged breakpoints (Empyrean Lord Lv10, Battlefield Banner Lv25). For 1v1 PvP, chase Executing Blade (Lv10, Lv25). For everyday PvX, the Battlefield Banner / Doom Shield / Nezekan Shield / Comrade in Arms breakpoints carry the most value.

🔩 Sub-stat Priority (Per Gear Piece)

Slotting the right sub-stats is what separates a Templar who tops the charts from one who just looks geared. A few mechanics decide everything before you even pick a stat:

  • Slot count: dungeon gear has 4 soulbind slots, crafted gear has 5.
  • Gold-gear transfer: if a piece is gold, you can transfer its sub-stats into crafted gear, which saves a huge amount of resources over rerolling.
  • Line order: stats with variable values (like Double Chance rolling 1.00% to 2.37%) belong in the first few soulbind lines, where the strong rolls land.
  • Tier-ups keep your work: the soulbind is retained when you tier up a piece (everything except the soul-fuse line), so binding early is a massive resource saving.
🧭 Crafted vs dungeon gear: the crafted table below is the finished target. On dungeon gear you steer toward one of two directions instead, both damage-first with a different secondary defensive layer. Pick the one that fits how you play.

Two Dungeon-Gear Builds

A) Damage + Evasion

Damage-focused with evasion passives as your secondary. This build pulls ahead against opponents who run lower accuracy, since more of their hits simply miss. A strong pick while the field is still under-geared.

B) Damage + Endurance

Damage-focused with endurance passives as your secondary. This is the stronger lategame choice: once you reach 100% endurance, the defensive value scales far better than evasion does.

🎯 Which one: run Evasion (A) early and against low-accuracy opponents; swap to Endurance (B) as you approach the point where you can cap endurance at 100%. The damage core is identical either way, so this is only a question of which defensive passives you lean on.

💰 Resource Traps - What Bricks New Accounts

Everything above is what to build. This section is how not to waste the resources getting there - the progression systems where new Templars burn kinah and materials they can't get back. Read this before you start dumping resources into anything.

⚠️ The golden rule: most of these systems retain what you invest when you tier up - but only if you invest in the right slot first. Bind early, bind smart, and you save weeks of farming. Bind blind and you pay for it twice.

🔩 Sub-stats & Soulbind (biggest resource saver)

How you soulbind gear decides whether your progression is cheap or brutal. The mechanics that matter:

  • Slot count: dungeon gear has 4 soulbind slots; crafted gear has 5.
  • Gold-gear transfer: if your piece is gold, you can transfer its sub-stats straight into crafted gear - this saves an enormous amount of resources versus rerolling from scratch.
  • Line order matters: stats with variable values (e.g. Double Chance 1.00% / 2.37%) should sit in the first few soulbind lines, where the good rolls land.
  • Tier-ups keep your work: when you tier up a piece, the soulbind is retained (except the soul-fuse line). Binding early = massive resource savings.
🔑 Bind early: the purple line in your gear UI is the fuse line - the one thing that does not carry on tier-up. Everything else you bind now follows the gear up the ladder, so don't wait.

💎 Skill Point Shards - don't skip these

💎 Grab every shard, early: collect all skill point shards as soon as you can from feathers, the nightmare shop, and every other source. These directly gate how fast you get Judgment, Punishment, and Pummel to Lv20 - the Holy Trinity this whole build is built on. Skipping them slows your entire power curve.

🃏 Arcana & Daevanion Board - Quick Adds

Arcana cards fix your skill allocation through RNG rolls, and you get them from Transcendence dungeons. Farm those once you are able.

⚔️ Go for damage in your set effects if you are not having trouble surviving. Survivability is rarely the Templar's problem, so unless you are actually dying, push your arcana set effects toward damage (like the Frenzy set: PvE Attack and Boss Damage Boost) rather than defensive lines.
Skill Priority by Arcana

Slot your key skills onto the arcana that carries them best. Aim for these first:

  • Chalice: Pummel / Punishment.
  • Parchment: Judgment / Punishment.
  • Compass: Pummel / Shield Smite.
  • Bell: Fury / Punishing Benediction.
Daevanion Board - What to Avoid Early
  • Skip MP nodes and reallocate those points to Attack.
  • Skip Flash Rampage (saves 3 points; it falls off past mid-game anyway).
  • Skip the green Warding Shield / Guardian Shield nodes unless you have spare points.
