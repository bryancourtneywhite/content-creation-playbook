🧩 Why Berserk Gladiator

Gladiator is a melee powerhouse, and the Berserk spec leans into raw damage and aggressive pressure. Here's what makes the build work:

  • High burst potential for opening fights and finishing kills
  • Strong gap-closing to stick to targets in PvP
  • Sustained rotation that translates well to PvE content
