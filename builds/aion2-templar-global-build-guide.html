🧰 Tools: NotMeter & ExitLag

Before the in-game build, two external tools supplement everything else and make the build more successful.

NotMeter - DPS Meter & Field Boss Tracker

NotMeter is both a DPS meter and a field boss timer tracker. Two reasons it matters for Templar (and every class):

  • Field boss tracking. Bosses like Eternal, Isle, Vertua, and Dartain drop gear - get two pieces and you turn it in for a Supply Request, which gives AP you spend on stigmas. Mid-to-end game you cannot afford to miss a boss, because missing a boss means missing stats.
  • DPS comparison. The in-game meter only shows your own DPS. NotMeter shows the rest of your party. In Transcendence content, one person with low DPS can fail the whole group - you need to see where everyone stands.
NotMeter Settings to Enable

🧩 Gear Philosophy: Stats Over Score

This is the single most important idea in the entire build, so it comes first. Do not stat-pad. You can inflate your combat power, item level, and gear score all you want, but if the stats are bad, it does not matter.

🧩 The rule: A blue with the right stats beats a gold with bad stats. This is the number one difference between Sol's build and most content creators - they stat-pad for the score, Sol builds for essential stats. Wear the piece with the best stats even if its item level is lower.
The Stats You Build On Every Piece
  • Block - your #1 defensive stat as Templar. Build it on every piece you can. Goal: ~1,000 block (you get +200 from Shield Rush and more from passives).
  • Accuracy - very important, you cannot hit people in PvP without it.
  • Critical Hit - very important for damage.
  • Perfect Resistance - very important defensive stat.
  • Status Effect Resistance - amplified by Willpower. Sol sits at ~60% base before buffs.
  • Willpower - "survival willpower" synergy, amplifies status effect resistance.

Endurance is good if you get it, but block is the main defensive stat. Evasion is fine - Sol runs it on several pieces - but he would prefer block given the choice.

✨ Skills & Loadouts

Sol runs three loadouts: PvE, PvP, and PvX. The leveling priority for your core DPS skills is fixed, and it also happens to be how you path your Daeva board.

DPS Skill Leveling Priority
  1. Pummel - your main DPS. You can miss-strike and Pummel your way through most of the quest line.
  2. Judgment
  3. Punishment
  4. Annihilate

Those are your 4 main DPS skills. Level them in that order.

PvE Loadout

PvE rotation (ideal opener): Shield Smite (triggers Judgment) → Debilitating Smash if it procs (lowers defense) → Judgment → Pummel cancel. Priority logic:

  • Annihilate takes priority - use it off cooldown, it does too much damage (more than Judgment, more than Debilitating Smash).
  • Debilitating Smash second - for the defense lower and Shield Smite spam.
  • Shield Smite typically procs off Debilitating Smash; Annihilate procs off status effect; Flash Rampage situational. When none are up, default to a Pummel.
  • After your two seconds of Shield Smite / Warning Strike, go to Doom Shield.
  • Battlefield Banner for the defense buff; Punishment off cooldown.
PvP Loadout
  • Executing Blade over Imperion - with Flex Expose (reduces block + evasion 5s). Executing Blade on a low target + Punishment DoT stops healing and secures kills on regen-heavy targets. Imperion is still good as an extra CC and better vs ranged; Executing Blade is better vs tanky opponents (and at Challenger, everyone is tanky).
  • Grapple - +20% shield and an extra engage. Also goated for group PvP: pulls 4 enemies into a clump for a Doom Shield knockdown → Annihilate reset → AoE Annihilate potential (insane at level 20).
  • Mobility chain: lead with Poach → Shield Rush (10s CD) → Doom Shield → by then Poach is back. Alternate mobility skills so you always have a CC, gap-closer, or pull available. Grapple backs up Poach if it fails.
PvX Loadout

Used when farming open world but needing to be PvP-ready. Rotation is built on Doom Shield: Doom Shield gets resets off mobs, heals off the mob, then back into combat. Rush to a mob and infinite-reset while PvE-ing.

Stigma Levels (Season 1 update)
  • Skills are now at the thresholds: +20 Judgment and +20 Punishment. At 20 Judgment, Debilitating Smash and Flash Rampage drop out of the DPS core (Flash Rampage stays a Nightmare-dungeon heal). Next target: 20 Pummel (PvP) or 20 Vicious Strike (pure-PvE animation cancel).
  • PvX: Nezekan's Shield 20 and Grapple 20 (20% PvE + 10% PvP damage tolerance when you pull groups into you - you get tankier by grouping enemies). Pushing 20 Doom Shield for the extra knockdown chance and Annihilate resets.
  • General rule: lead with your defensive stigmas for PvP - block/mitigate the opponent's initial burst, alternate CCs, and only commit damage when they are out of stamina or CC'd. Templar wins by countering and predicting, not by brute force.
The Season 1 PvP stigma package (the big change)

The PvP loadout is very different from before. The core is the Second Skin + Nezekan's survivability stack:

  • Second Skin - pop it early in a fight for ~20s: block jumps from ~1,100 to ~1,371, plus a 100% damage-reduction window, multiplicative Ironclad Defense (more endurance), increased PvE + PvP damage tolerance, and Elemental Tolerance (the kit's only elemental defense). This is what makes Templar nearly unkillable.
  • Nezekan's - the heal + damage tolerance. In a group, swap Second Skin → Comrade in Arms to shield/heal your whole party (party shield + elemental tolerance + damage tolerance); solo small-scale, keep Second Skin + Nezekan's.
  • Shoulder Protection - block on command, extra move speed, and a ~10% heal every time you block. Swap it for Taunt in PvE when your team is the one getting focused.
  • Doom Shield - extra block + Annihilate resets → more knockdown potential.
Rotation & the 25ms macro
  • PvE opener: Battlefield Banner → Noble Armor → Battlefield Banner → Punishment → Imperial Lord's → Poach → Shield Smite → Judgment, weaving 2s of Judgment off each of Warning Strike / Shield Rush and 3s off Doom Shield (~122-128K DPS in testing).
  • Macro delay: run a 25ms macro, not the default 10ms. At ~43-45 ping that landed more animation cancels and more damage - tune the ms to your own latency (read your ping in ExitLag). Annihilate on right-click (falls through to Flash Rampage → Pummel); Judgment on the Razer Hypershift / R key (falls through to Shield Smite → Warning Strike, Turbo 20s).
  • Hold vs weave: with under ~50% combat speed, quick-cast Punishment after the second rotation instead of fully charging; at 60-70%+ it is instant enough to full-charge mid-combo. Test on the dummy whether holding Judgment or manually animation-cancelling into Pummel does more for your combat speed.
  • Skill-point threshold tip: it is not worth pushing a skill past a threshold you can't complete - stop at 12 if you can't reach 16, stop at 16 if you can't reach 20. Dump spare points into Annihilate (more damage than Shield Smite/Poach).

🎚️ Skill Specialties

Specialties change per game mode. Below are the choices that actually matter - the rest default once you hit level 20 and can take all three options.

PvE Specialties
  • Pummel: Multi Hit + Warding Strike cooldown (procs more Judgments) + Absorb HP.
  • Punishing Strike: extra damage on the second hit - weave both punishing-strike hits into your Judgment combo (combat-speed dependent).
  • Shield Rush: Move to Target on bosses as a mobility skill; reset cooldown only for open world. In a dungeon, go Max HP Protective Shield (start the fight with a shield).
  • Debilitating Smash: 50% chance to trigger is necessary - this is the defense reduction that starts your combo and drives your damage. Take the negative cooldown.
  • Judgment: Critical Hit + Remove Cooldown. The extra damage is better than crit if you crit; if you don't crit, crit is better - subjective until you have all three at level 20.
  • Flash Rampage: MP Restore at low level is nice; Multi Hit is nice; can swap to HP for survivability.
  • Debilitate: for pure PvE, take Debilitate Duration (keeps the defense debuff on the boss longer) over incoming-heal (which is more a PvX pick).
  • Punishment: inflicts DoT + 30% skill speed. The DoT does an extra ~20k - roughly 50% extra damage since the base hit is only ~40k. DoT also helps hold aggro.
  • Shield Rush (continued): cooldown reduction + extra stamina (more dodges / survivability in Transcendence) + 100% damage to targets with incapacitated immunity (i.e. bosses).
  • Defiance: remove stun → extra stamina + HP. Damage tolerance is great at level 16 (not there yet).
PvP Specialties
  • Pummel: Absorb HP + Multi Hit (breaks shields) + deal more damage.
  • Punishing Strike: extra damage on hit (12% + flat) is clutch - with enough combat speed you fit more punishing strikes into the weave. At level 17 go for the extra damage + absorption; you'll want all three eventually.
  • Poach: Status Effect Resistance + Max HP.
  • Shield Smite: cooldown + Debilitating Smash.
  • Judgment: Critical Hit + Remove Cooldown (not used for PvP).
  • Debilitating Smash: take the healing reduction - absolutely necessary for PvP; ignore block/evasion lands as multi hit is also good.
  • Warding Strike: same as PvE.
  • Shield Rush: take the 10m range (not extra stamina) - extra engage so you don't get kited.
  • Annihilate: Knockdown Chance + Cooldown Reduction - absolutely want this.
  • Punishment: cooldown speed + DoT - 20k extra damage and keeps targets in combat (goated). If you want to one-shot, ignore block/evasion lands as crit is better.
  • Capture Change: extra mobility if CC'd; better vs ranged. Vs melee, Tenacity (status effect chance reduction) may be better.
PvX Specialties

Mostly the same as PvP - the main difference is the stigmas, not the specialties. Sol runs essentially the same spec between PvX and PvP; only the stigma selection changes.

🌟 Daeva Board Pathing

You will eventually get every node, so this is about pathing order when you are new. The rule is simple: always path toward your main DPS skills first, then your main DPS passives. The 5 board layouts (Azphel, Nezekan, Triniel, Vaizel, Zikel) are on the global build page.

Pathing Order
  1. DPS skills first: Pummel → Judgment → Punishment → Annihilate (grab the gold nodes along the way). Starting from Nezekan you'd path to Judgment, then Pummel, then the gold node.
  2. Main DPS passives next:
    • Fury
    • Punishing Benediction (slightly better for PvP)
    • Insulting Roar (slightly better for PvE)
    • Impact Hit - very important (double chance + impact-type chance)
  3. Then survivability: Survival Willpower and Ironclad Defense (congruently important - damage tolerance + defense/endurance).
  4. Then: Block Pain (PvP damage tolerance + flat HP).
  5. Last priority: Guardian Seal, then Warding Shield.
🌟 Node by node. Sol did not have this mapped out at first - he went node to node toward the nearest DPS skill. Whichever starting deity you have, aim at the closest DPS skill first (usually Pummel or Judgment), grab the gold node near it, and keep walking toward the next DPS skill. The passive order above is what you prioritize once the 4 DPS skills are secured.
