🆓 Free First

Free ping fixes (do these first)

Before paying for anything, these cost nothing and often make the biggest difference.

🔌

Go wired

Ethernet over Wi-Fi is the single biggest win. Wi-Fi adds jitter and packet loss that wreck PvP timing.

🌍

Pick the right server

Play on the region closest to you. A lower base ping beats any optimizer trying to fix a far server.

📉

Kill background bandwidth

Pause cloud sync, downloads, and other devices streaming 4K. Bandwidth spikes = lag spikes.

⚙️

Enable QoS on your router

Prioritize your gaming PC's traffic in the router settings so game packets go first.

🔄

Update network drivers

Old NIC drivers cause latency and disconnects. Grab the latest from your motherboard/NIC vendor.

🧊

Close overlays

Some overlays and capture tools add input and network overhead. Trim what you don't need while competing.
