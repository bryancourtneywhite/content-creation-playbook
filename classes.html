TankLongsword

Templar

Role: Main tank / durable PvX. Weapon: Longsword.

Playstyle: The anchor — high survivability, party protection, and steady pressure. Rewards players who like holding the line. In the current meta a strong group class; my main.

PvE: reliable tank & group anchor. PvP: god-mode PvX durability with real burst when built for DPS.

Gladiator

Role: Heavy melee bruiser. Weapon: Greatsword.

Playstyle: Hits hard without full-tanking — big burst and cleave, easy to make an impact with. Consistently rated one of the strongest all-around picks.

PvE: top-tier damage & cleave. PvP: huge burst; the build that carried me to Rank 1.

Melee DPSDagger

Assassin

Role: Burst melee assassin. Weapon: Dagger.

Playstyle: High-mobility burst — dodge everything, delete a target, reset. High skill ceiling, elite in good hands. Widely rated one of the best PvP picks.

PvE: solid single-target once geared. PvP: premier duelist / ganker.

Ranger

Role: Ranged physical all-rounder. Weapon: Bow.

Playstyle: Safe, consistent ranged damage with strong kiting. Beginner-friendly and one of the strongest PvE picks by leaderboard data.

PvE: reliable top-end DPS. PvP: strong kiter that punishes overextension.

Ranged DPSSpellbook

Sorcerer

Role: Magic glass cannon. Weapon: Spellbook.

Playstyle: Big spells from range with massive AoE — shines in PvE packs. High damage, low survivability, so positioning is everything.

PvE: excellent AoE clear. PvP: high burst but fragile — needs peel.

Ranged DPSOrb

Spiritmaster (a.k.a. Elementalist)

Role: Pet-based summoner. Weapon: Orb.

Playstyle: A pet does much of the work while you sustain damage from range. Sources rate it a strong PvE DPS pick; positioning and pet management define the ceiling.

PvE: often a top DPS recommendation. PvP: situational; strong with good pet control.

Playstyle: The must-have group healer — keeps the party alive through the hardest content. Beginner-friendly, always in demand for groups.

PvE: essential group anchor. PvP: strong in organized groups, weaker solo.
